License¶

Summary¶

CeCILL-B is a French free software license, compatible with the BSD license family. Key points:

  • Free to use, modify, and redistribute — for both academic and commercial purposes
  • Strong attribution obligation — you must credit the original authors in any redistribution
  • No copyleft — modified versions may be distributed under CeCILL-B, CeCILL-C, or compatible licenses
  • French law — designed for compliance with French intellectual property law
